This budget prioritizes access over ownership, community engagement over isolation, and strategic professional development over immediate production maximization.

## TIPS FOR STRENGTHENING YOUR APPLICATION

### In the Narrative:
- **Be specific about Holophage**: Add 1-2 sentences clearly explaining what the series is about conceptually. Reviewers need to understand your work quickly.
- **Show artistic trajectory**: Emphasize that this is about growth and evolution, not just maintaining current practice
- **Connect to Chicago**: Mention specific venues, festivals, or communities you're engaging with
- **Be concrete**: Instead of "larger scale," mention dimensions or specific technical specs if possible

### In the Budget:
- **Be realistic**: Use actual prices from rental houses, stores, or average festival fees in Chicago
- **Show research**: Budget should reflect that you've actually priced things out
- **Justify the mix**: The balance between production, research, and professional development shows you're thinking holistically

### For Work Samples:
- **Quality over quantity**: 5 excellent samples beats 8 mediocre ones
- **Clear titles and descriptions**: Help reviewers understand what they're looking at
- **Show your best**: This is not the place for experiments that didn't work
- **Video length**: Keep excerpts tight (1-3 minutes) unless the full piece is essential

### General:
- **Write clearly**: Avoid excessive jargon; write for intelligent non-specialists
- **Proofread**: Typos signal lack of care
- **Be yourself**: Let your authentic artistic voice come through
- **Show momentum**: Convey that you're actively working and this grant will accelerate existing momentum
